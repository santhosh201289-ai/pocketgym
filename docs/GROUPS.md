# Groups and coaching

A coach can run groups on a Pocket Gym server: add people, see how they train, and send them
routines. Nothing reaches a member's plan until that member accepts it.

## Who can coach

An admin is always a coach. An admin can make anyone else a coach under **Admin dashboard →
Users → (the person) → Make coach**. **Remove coach role** keeps that person's existing groups,
but they can't make new ones.

## For a coach

Go to **Settings → Account → Groups & coaching**.

1. **New group:** give it a name, for example "Morning batch".
2. **Add people:** search members of this server by name. Each person gets an invitation and
   decides whether to join. Until they join, you see nothing of theirs.
3. **Members:** tap an active member to see:
   - their workouts from the last 12 weeks: each set's weight and reps, and whether it was done
   - how many times a week they train, and their weekly plan
   - their steps, sleep and cardio from the last 30 days
   - their body weight from the last 6 months

   You never see notes, progress photos, settings or sign-in details.
4. **Send a routine:** pick one of your own routines and choose when it should be trained:
   - weekdays, to train it every week, or
   - dates, to train it once (that day's workout)

   Send it to everyone in the group or to particular people, and add a note if you like. The
   **Sent** list shows who accepted it. If you change the routine and send it again, it updates
   the member's copy rather than adding a second one.

## For a member

When there's something waiting, Home shows a card; you can also go to **Settings → Account →
Groups & coaching**.

- **Invitation:** joining lets the coach see your workouts, activity and body weight, and send
  you routines. **Decline** if you'd rather not.
- **Routine from your coach:** you see it, its days and anything it would replace, then choose
  **Accept** or **Decline**. When you accept, it's added to your routines marked as your coach's,
  with weights converted to your unit, and it goes on the chosen days. You can edit or delete it
  like any routine.
- **Leaving:** tap the group and choose **Leave group**. The coach stops seeing your training.
  Routines you already accepted stay in your plan.

## Plan by voice

Go to **Plan → This week → Plan by voice**, then say or type what you want:

- "Add a push workout on Tuesday": puts Push on every Tuesday.
- "Legs every Monday and Thursday"
- "Pull day tomorrow" or "full body next Friday": that day only.
- "Rest on Sunday": clears Sunday.

The workout is matched to one of your routines by name, or by kind: push, pull, legs, upper,
lower, full body or 5×5. If you have no routine of that kind, a starter one is made and fitted to
your equipment. If a day already has a routine, you choose whether to replace it or train both.

On the web, voice uses the browser's own speech recognition (Chrome, Edge or Safari); on Android
it uses the phone's. Commands are in English. Where speech isn't available, type instead.
